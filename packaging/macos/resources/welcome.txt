This installs Dussek: a physically modeled piano, harpsichords, harps and percussion, as a standalone (Dussek in Applications) and as LV2, CLAP and VST3 plugins and an AudioUnit for Logic Pro and GarageBand (in /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins). The instruments go to /Library/Application Support/Dussek.

Weidinger, a choice of its own (Customize), is the winds: trumpet, horn, tenor and bass trombone, tuba and clarinet, physically modeled from the same sources, as its own standalone and plugins. A preview: its sound and controls may still change.

Where a host offers LV2 or CLAP, prefer them to the VST3.

The macOS build runs on Apple silicon and Intel Macs. Reports welcome at https://github.com/ccoors/dussek/issues

The package is not signed by a registered developer: macOS asked you to confirm before opening it (right-click, Open; or System Settings, Privacy & Security, Open Anyway).

To remove them, delete Dussek and Weidinger from Applications, dussek.lv2, dussek.clap, dussek.vst3, dussek.component and weidinger.lv2, weidinger.clap, weidinger.vst3, weidinger.component from /Library/Audio/Plug-Ins, and /Library/Application Support/Dussek.
